Engagement models

Flexible engagement.
Clear ownership.

Choose the commercial shape that matches the certainty, scale and life stage of the product. Every route keeps scope, responsibilities and decision points visible.

01

Reduce uncertainty

Discovery & Product Blueprint

A focused engagement to turn a broad brief into an actionable product and technical plan. Useful before committing to a major build or when several approaches appear possible.

Typical outputs

  • User and workflow map
  • Scope and priority definition
  • Technical architecture
  • Risk and dependency register
  • Delivery roadmap and estimate basis

Best for

  • New products with important unknowns
  • Legacy replacement or integration projects
  • Investor or stakeholder-ready planning
  • Complex game or Web3 systems
02

Defined delivery

Fixed-Scope Product Build

A milestone-led build for a product or release with clear boundaries. Scope, acceptance criteria, dependencies and change control are agreed before implementation begins.

Typical structure

  • Agreed deliverables and milestones
  • Regular demonstrations
  • Quality and acceptance plan
  • Deployment and documentation
  • Optional post-launch support

Best for

  • Minimum viable products
  • Vertical slices and prototypes
  • Defined platform or feature builds
  • Time-bound campaign experiences
03

Evolving roadmap

Embedded Studio Partnership

Reserved monthly capacity for products that need continuing design, engineering and technical leadership. Priorities can evolve through a shared backlog without restarting procurement for every release.

Typical structure

  • Reserved multidisciplinary capacity
  • Joint roadmap and backlog
  • Regular planning and demonstrations
  • Architecture and delivery ownership
  • Monthly reporting

Best for

  • Active digital products
  • Longer game productions
  • Complex phased platforms
  • Teams needing senior delivery support
04

After launch

Maintenance & Evolution Subscription

A structured monthly service covering operational care, bug resolution, dependency updates and a defined allowance for improvements. Support levels can scale with product criticality.

Typical coverage

  • Monitoring and issue triage
  • Security and dependency updates
  • Release and platform maintenance
  • Small enhancements
  • Health and roadmap reviews

Best for

  • Products without an internal engineering team
  • Client-owned systems needing continuity
  • Games requiring live updates
  • Platforms exposed to changing APIs
