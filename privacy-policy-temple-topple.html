Privacy Supplement.

How Temple Topple handles local progress, settings and support requests.

Last updated: 23 October 2026
Applies to Android version 1.03 (version code 103), the closed-testing release.
App identifier: com.aumorastudios.templetopple

This product notice supplements the Aumora Studios Privacy Policy and explains the specific practices of Temple Topple, a single-player mobile game from Aumora Studios. It applies to the Android release identified above; other games or later releases may have different features.

Temple Topple plays offline. Your progress and settings stay in the game's storage on your device. This release has no ads, in-app purchases, game accounts, chat, analytics SDKs or developer-operated game servers. Aumora does not receive personal information from gameplay. If you contact us separately, we receive the information you choose to send.

Local progress and settings

Temple Topple saves the following in its private app storage on your device:

  • Your best star rating and fewest shots for each completed level, used to restore campaign progress.
  • Audio volume and graphics preferences, including resolution, texture and effects quality, shadows and frame-rate settings.
  • Whether the local device-performance check has run and how the selected quality settings were chosen.

The device-performance check measures how the game runs on your device to choose suitable graphics settings. It runs locally; its measurements are not sent to Aumora or an analytics provider.

Progress and settings files are not uploaded to Aumora Studios or a game server. The game has no cloud-save or account-sync service.

This release disables Android's standard cloud app backup. On some devices, operating-system transfer tools may still move app data between devices; these are controlled by Android and your device manufacturer, rather than an Aumora cloud-save service. See Android's backup and device-transfer guidance.

Permissions, advertising and third-party services

The Android release requests no Android permissions. It does not request internet access, Advertising ID access, location, contacts, camera, microphone or shared-storage access. It includes no advertising, billing or analytics SDKs and does not send gameplay or diagnostic telemetry to Aumora.

Google Play, Android and your device manufacturer may independently handle information when you download, update, transfer or use apps, including platform diagnostics where enabled. Their own policies and your platform settings govern that processing. See Google's Privacy Policy for Google's practices.

Opening the studio privacy-policy link uses your browser. Visiting that website or sending an email is separate from the game's offline operation and is covered by the relevant parts of the studio privacy policy.

Support messages

If you email games@aumorastudios.com, we receive your email address, message and anything you attach. We use this information to respond, investigate a problem and resolve your request. Please avoid sending passwords, sensitive information or unnecessary personal information about a child.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle and follow up on the request and meet applicable recordkeeping or legal obligations. You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information we hold. The studio policy explains the purposes and lawful bases and your privacy rights.

Retention, deletion and your choices

  • Local progress and settings remain on your device until removed. To clear them, open Android Settings → Apps → Temple Topple → Storage → Clear storage (sometimes called Clear data). Menu names vary by device. This removes saved progress and settings; clearing only the cache is not the same action.
  • Uninstalling normally removes the game's local app data. Any copies made by device-transfer or other platform tools must be managed separately through those tools.
  • There is no Temple Topple online account or Aumora cloud save to delete. Because we do not hold your local saves, we cannot access, recover or remotely erase them.
  • Clearing storage or uninstalling does not delete an email you sent us or information independently held by a platform provider. Contact us about support correspondence, and use the provider's privacy options for information it controls.
  • You can play without an internet connection. Downloading or updating the game, opening a web link or sending support email may require a connection through the relevant platform or app.

Children's privacy

This release does not ask players for names, email addresses, dates of birth or account details. It has no chat, public profiles, ads or in-app purchases. These are statements about this release's features; audience suitability and age guidance are provided in its store listing.

A parent or guardian can contact us about a child's privacy or request help with information provided in a support message. Include the game name and the nature of the concern without sending unnecessary information about the child. The studio policy's children's privacy provisions also apply.

Changes to this release

Ads and purchases are planned for a later release and are not enabled in the version described here. Before introducing these or other features that change how information is handled, such as accounts, cloud saves or analytics, we will update this supplement and relevant store disclosures, provide appropriate notice and obtain consent where required. This notice does not describe those future features as already active.
