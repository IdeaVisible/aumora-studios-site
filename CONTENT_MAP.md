# Aumora Studios content map

## Positioning sentence

Aumora Studios is a multidisciplinary software house delivering custom applications, Web3 products, video games and interactive experiences, either as defined one-off builds or continuing engineering and maintenance partnerships.

- Reach Out (button to the contact form)

## Core message hierarchy

- **What Aumora is:** a product and engineering studio, not a generic agency or single-tool contractor.
- **What Aumora builds:** substantial custom digital products across four connected practices.
- **How clients can buy:** discovery, fixed-scope delivery, embedded monthly capacity, or a maintenance subscription.
- **Why the breadth is credible:** one product mindset, direct technical ownership, durable architecture and current studio projects showing system depth.
- **What is intentionally absent:** digital-download production. It is unrelated to the client proposition and should remain separate.

## Recommended case-study format

For each future client project, use:

- The starting problem
- The users and operating context
- Aumora's responsibility
- The delivered system
- Difficult technical or product decisions
- Measurable outcome
- Ongoing support relationship, if applicable

Avoid leading with a long technology list. Put technology in a compact secondary section after the outcome.
