Experience design

Attention is earned through agency.

We begin with the behaviour the experience should invite, then design the mechanics, feedback and technology around it.

01

Invite

Make the first action obvious, low-friction and immediately rewarding.

02

Deepen

Introduce discovery, progression, social proof or challenge without burying the purpose.

03

Continue

Create a reason to return, share, learn more or move into the next stage of the journey.

A good fit when

The audience needs to feel involved, not merely informed.

  • A brand wants a digital activation with measurable participation.
  • Training or education content needs stronger engagement and feedback.
  • A community or creator product needs a distinctive shared mechanic.
  • A complex proposition is easier to understand through simulation.
  • An event needs an interactive layer that survives beyond the venue.

Experience brief

Tell us what the audience should do, feel or understand differently.
