Privacy Policy.

How we handle personal information across Aumora's games, apps, websites, software and services.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Aumora Studios ("Aumora", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information that identifies you or can be linked to you, and how you can ask questions or exercise your privacy rights.

This is Aumora's general privacy policy for all games, applications, websites, software, interactive experiences and related services that we develop, publish, own or operate. The information handled depends on the product and features you use. A product may work entirely on your device; another may use accounts, online features, advertising or other services. Details specific to a product are provided alongside this policy.

Scope and responsibility

This policy covers our current and future products and services, including mobile, desktop, console, browser and other platforms, free and paid releases, demos, prototypes, beta tests, updates and downloadable content. It also covers communications with us, including support and business enquiries. We refer to these collectively as the "Services".

Aumora Studios is responsible for personal information where we decide why and how it is used (the "data controller" under applicable data protection laws). If we build or maintain a product for another organisation and handle information only on its instructions, that organisation's privacy notice governs its use of the information. Creating software for a client does not make Aumora responsible for every use the client makes of it.

Read this policy together with any notice shown in the relevant product, store listing or feature. A more specific notice explains the practices for that Service and takes precedence on the matters it expressly covers. Third-party platforms and services have their own responsibilities, as explained below.

Information we handle

Not every Service collects every category below. Depending on the features you use, information may come directly from you, from your device when you use a Service, or from a platform or provider involved in delivering that feature:

  • Information you provide: your name, email address, business details, support messages, feedback, bug reports and attachments when you contact us or submit them through an enabled feature.
  • Account and platform information: a username, user or player ID, profile details, authentication information and preferences where accounts, sign-in or platform integrations are available.
  • Product and gameplay information: saved progress, settings, scores, achievements, usage events, content you submit and interactions with online features. Some of this may remain entirely on your device.
  • Technical information: device type, operating system, browser or app version, language, IP address, service logs, crash reports and performance information. An IP address can indicate an approximate location.
  • Purchases and subscriptions: transaction identifiers, receipts, purchase status and entitlements where payments are supported. The payment provider or app store handles the payment details you give it under its own privacy notice.
  • Advertising and consent information: available device or advertising identifiers, ad views and interactions, and your consent or privacy choices where advertising or related measurement is used.

Additional data, such as location, camera images, microphone recordings or selected files, is relevant only where a feature uses it. The feature's notice and permission request explain what is needed and why. Please avoid sending sensitive information or another person's information that is not needed for your request.

How and why we use information

We use information relevant to a Service to deliver its features, save or synchronise progress where supported, manage accounts and purchases, answer enquiries, resolve faults, understand performance, prevent fraud and abuse, protect users and meet legal obligations. Advertising, optional measurement and marketing apply only where offered and subject to the choices and legal requirements that apply to them.

Where UK or European data protection law applies, the basis for processing depends on the purpose:

  • Contract: information necessary to provide a Service you request, fulfil a purchase or take steps you ask for before entering an agreement.
  • Legitimate interests: responding to general enquiries, diagnosing faults, securing our Services and preventing misuse, where these interests are not outweighed by your rights and interests.
  • Consent: optional activities for which consent is required, such as certain advertising, tracking or marketing. You can withdraw consent through the relevant controls or by contacting us.
  • Legal obligations: required records, lawful requests and other duties imposed by law.

We explain the applicable purpose and basis in a product or collection notice where further detail is needed. If information is necessary to deliver a requested feature and you choose not to provide it, that feature may be unavailable. Using a Service or reading this policy does not by itself give consent to optional processing.

Local storage, permissions and online features

Games and apps may store progress, preferences, downloaded content or other files locally. Local storage alone does not mean those files are sent to Aumora. A Service's notice explains whether it also uses accounts, cloud saves, multiplayer, leaderboards or other online features. Device backups and transfers may be managed separately by your operating system or platform.

You can manage supported permissions in your device or browser settings. Disabling a permission may prevent a feature from working. Offline use, reset options and account deletion differ between products; check the relevant settings or product notice.

If you use public or shared features, such as a leaderboard, profile or community space, information you choose to share may be visible to other people. Where a Web3 feature records information on a public blockchain, wallet addresses and transactions may be public and permanent; deleting an app or requesting deletion from Aumora cannot erase the underlying blockchain record.

Advertising, analytics and cookies

Some Services may include ads, optional rewarded ads, analytics or crash reporting; others may include none of these. The relevant product notice identifies the providers and explains the data involved and available controls. A provider's software may transmit information directly from your device to that provider.

For example, where a product uses Google Mobile Ads, Google may process IP addresses, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Non-personalised or test ads do not necessarily mean that no information is processed. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps and sites using its services.

Websites and online features may use cookies or similar storage for essential functionality, preferences and, where enabled, measurement or advertising. Where consent is required, optional technologies must remain off until you consent. Available consent controls let you refuse or change those choices. Browser settings can also manage cookies, although blocking essential storage can affect functionality.

Device and platform settings may offer further advertising controls. Turning off personalised ads does not necessarily stop all ads or processing needed to deliver them, keep them secure or prevent fraud. Any sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, where those terms apply under local law, must be disclosed in the relevant Service's notice with the required opt-out choices.

Sharing and third-party services

Where relevant to the Service, information may be shared with:

  • Service providers supporting hosting, storage, email, customer support, diagnostics or other operations, under appropriate instructions and safeguards when acting on our behalf.
  • Platform and feature providers supplying sign-in, app distribution, payments, advertising or other integrations you use. Some act independently under their own privacy policies.
  • Other users or recipients you choose when you use sharing, public or collaborative features.
  • Authorities and professional advisers where necessary to meet a legal obligation, protect rights or safety, investigate misuse or establish or defend legal claims.
  • A successor organisation if a relevant business or Service is transferred, subject to applicable law, appropriate safeguards and notice where required.

Product notices identify relevant integrations. Following an external link or using an app store, payment service or platform may involve processing outside Aumora's control. Their policies explain their own practices; this does not remove our responsibilities for information we control.

Children's privacy

The intended audience and available features vary by product. A store age rating is not a statement that every Aumora Service has the same privacy practices or minimum age. Check the product's age guidance and privacy notice for any child-specific settings, advertising restrictions or parental controls.

Where a Service is directed to children or is likely to be accessed by them, its design and information handling must take account of the protections required by applicable law. Where parental permission is legally required, it must be obtained before the relevant collection or use. A general statement in this policy does not replace that permission.

A parent or guardian can contact us to ask about a child's information, request access or deletion, or raise a concern. If you believe information has been collected from a child without required permission, tell us which Service is involved so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

Retention and deletion

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for its purpose, including any necessary legal, accounting, security or dispute-related period. Retention depends on the type of information, the Service, whether an account remains active, the status of a support request and applicable obligations. When it is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymised.

  • Local data: use the product's reset or deletion controls, or your device's app-storage controls where available. Uninstalling may remove local files, but platform backups can remain under your platform settings.
  • Accounts and cloud data: use the Service's deletion options or contact us. Uninstalling an app alone does not delete a server account, cloud save, support message or purchase record.
  • Support correspondence: retained for handling and following up on the request and any necessary recordkeeping or dispute resolution.
  • Provider-held information: independent providers set their own retention periods and request procedures. We remain responsible for handling requests about information processed on our behalf.

Deletion may be limited where information must be retained by law or is needed for a valid legal claim. Backup copies may remain until the applicable backup cycle replaces them, with their use restricted. Contact us for the retention criteria or period relevant to your Service and request.

Security and international processing

We use safeguards appropriate to the information and Service, including limiting access to people and providers who need it. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device and account credentials, and contact us if you suspect a security issue involving your information.

Services and their providers may process information in countries other than your own, where privacy laws can differ. Where a transfer of information we control requires legal safeguards, we use the applicable transfer mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or approved contractual safeguards. You can contact us for details of relevant destinations and safeguards, including a copy where available.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Know how your information is used and request access or a copy.
  • Correct inaccurate information or request deletion.
  • Restrict processing or object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Receive certain information in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.
  • Object to direct marketing, and opt out of relevant sales, sharing or targeted advertising where local law provides that right.
  • Request applicable safeguards, including human review where required, for decisions based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects. A Service using such decisions must explain them in its notice.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

These rights have conditions and exceptions. Contact us using the details below; we may need proportionate information to verify your identity or authority to act for someone else. We respond within the period required by applicable law and explain any lawful limit on a request. Where local law provides an appeal right, you can ask us to review a decision using the same contact details. Exercising a privacy right will not result in unlawful discrimination.

You can also use available in-product settings, device permissions, consent controls and unsubscribe options. If an independent provider controls the information, its privacy tools may be the most direct route; you can still contact us for help identifying the responsible party.
