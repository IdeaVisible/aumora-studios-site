Aumora Studios · Pew Pew Vroom Vroom

Privacy Policy.

How the game handles local progress, advertising data and support requests.

Last updated: 18 September 2026
Applies to version 1.03 and its closed-testing release.

Pew Pew Vroom Vroom is a single-player Android game provided by Aumora Studios. This policy explains how information is handled when you play, use advertising features or contact us.

Your game progress stays on your device. No game account is required. The game includes Google Mobile Ads, which processes some device, network, advertising-interaction and diagnostic information. Gameplay is available offline; ads require an internet connection.

Game progress and settings

The game saves progress, in-game resources, upgrades, records, reward receipts and settings in its private storage on your device. The game does not send these save files to Aumora Studios or a game server. Android backup and device-transfer features, if applicable, are controlled by your device and operating-system settings.

Advertising and third-party services

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show full-screen ads between runs and optional rewarded ads. In the closed-testing release, these placements use Google's demo ads and do not generate advertising revenue.

Even when an ad is a test ad or is not personalized, the SDK may collect and share information with Google, including:

  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate an approximate location.
  • App and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as launch time, errors, hangs and energy usage.
  • Device, app-set or other identifiers available to the SDK, subject to the settings described below.

Google documents these categories as used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Aumora Studios does not receive your local save files through this integration. No separate analytics SDK or game-account system is included.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Children and advertising settings

The game's intended audience includes players aged 9 and older. This version applies child-directed treatment and under-age-of-consent settings to all ad requests, requests non-personalized ads, and sets the maximum ad content rating to G. It does not request Android Advertising ID access or precise or approximate device-location permissions. An IP address may still reveal an approximate location.

The game does not ask players for names, dates of birth, email addresses or other account details. It has no chat, user profiles or in-app purchasing. Parents or guardians can contact us about privacy concerns at games@aumorastudios.com.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include. We use this information to respond and resolve your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information or children's personal information that is not needed for support. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. You may contact us to request its deletion.

Retention, deletion and your choices

  • Local progress remains on your device until removed. Use Settings → Reset All Progress in the game to reset progress. Android's Clear storage action removes the app's local data and settings.
  • There is no Aumora Studios game account to delete. Resetting progress does not delete information already processed by Google or an email you have sent us.
  • Google controls retention and deletion of information it processes under its own privacy policy. Its privacy tools and contact options are described in that policy.
  • You can play without an internet connection. Ads and optional ad rewards are unavailable offline.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of personal information. Contact us for requests concerning information Aumora Studios holds, such as support correspondence. For information held by Google, use the options in Google's privacy policy.

Permissions and security

The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, shared photos or files, or device location. Network-related permissions support advertising. We limit game data to local storage, and Google states that its SDK's data transfers use encryption. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Policy updates

We will update this page when the game's data practices change, including before introducing materially different advertising, accounts, analytics or purchasing features. The date above identifies the latest update.
