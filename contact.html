The strongest first brief explains the problem, the people affected, the constraints and the outcome. We can help shape the product and technical route from there.

Useful context

A concise brief is enough to begin.

Include what you know and leave the uncertain parts uncertain. Discovery exists to turn those unknowns into decisions.

  • What is currently difficult, slow or impossible?
  • Who will use or operate the product?
  • What systems, platforms or constraints already exist?
  • Is there a target date or commercial deadline?
  • Do you need a one-off build or ongoing product capacity?
