Tower defence · RobloxLive

PiCO Defenders: Toy Tower Defense

A toy-themed tower defence game on Roblox, built around conveyor-belt battles and upgradeable machines.

Build defences along branching routes, counter flying, speedy and armoured toys, and take on bigger bosses. Coins power each run, while gems unlock permanent improvements that carry into the next battle.

Aumora focus

  • Conveyor-based enemy paths and branching routes
  • Upgradeable defence machines and enemy counters
  • Run-based currency and permanent progression
  • Level goals, boss encounters and replayability

Social sandbox · RobloxLive

PiCO Paradise

An open-world social sandbox on Roblox, with personal islands, crafting, exploration and connected adventures.

Players build and upgrade their island homes, travel by boat, craft equipment and discover loot and secrets. Connected destinations bring shooting, obstacle-course challenges and deep-sea exploration into the same persistent player journey.

Aumora focus

  • Cross-place architecture and persistent player data
  • Island upgrades, crafting, quests and inventory systems
  • Connected game modes and exploration
  • Interface, onboarding and player feedback systems

Competitive card game · RobloxLive

PiCO Card Game

A fast best-of-three card battler on Roblox, built around one weapon, one shield and one character per round.

The design combines clear battle rules with collection, crafting, colour bonuses, character risk and competitive progression. Supporting systems include card packs, special cards, daily rewards and weekly tournaments.

Aumora focus

  • Core rules, battle flow and state management
  • Collection, crafting and reward progression
  • Competitive tables, dungeon and runner modes
  • Weekly tournaments and reward claims

TILLERIONBEYOND THE QUIET MACHINES
Narrative 2D side-scroller · GodotPre-production

Tillerion

A narrative side-scroller set among overgrown ruins, abandoned farms and sterile machine-controlled environments.

The project explores a small expressive machine travelling through a post-collapse world where older human systems and autonomous technology have grown apart. Production planning centres on reusable 2D systems, modular environments and strong visual storytelling.

Aumora focus

  • World, character and gameplay concept development
  • Godot architecture and reusable scene systems
  • 2D environment, parallax and asset pipeline planning
  • Movement, interaction and narrative delivery design
